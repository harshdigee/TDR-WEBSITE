## 11. Blog Posts

Blog content is **not hard-coded** in the repository. It is fetched dynamically from the backend API (`${BaseUrl}/api/blog` and `${BaseUrl}/api/blog/${blog_slug}`).
- **Extracted Fields**: `title`, `created_at`, `slug`, `thumbnail`, `banner_image`, `content` (HTML), `faq`.
- **Layouts**: `BlogBanner.js` (Listing Hero), `BlogItems.js` (Cards), `BlogDetail.js` (Post View), `RelatedBlogs.js` (Carousel).
